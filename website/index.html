Questions

Good to know

How do I install the app from this website?
  1. Tap “Download for Android” on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. Tap Install, then open Darji Parivar.
Is it free?

Yes. Creating a profile, liking, favourites and chatting with your connections are free. Premium (₹99/month) unlocks full profiles, all photos and contact details.

Who can see my photos?

Your main photo appears on your card. You can turn on “Only connections see my photo” in Account so others see just your initials until you both like each other.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open Google Play → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions, choose Darji Parivar and cancel. Premium stays active until the end of the month you paid for.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Account and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, photos, likes and messages.
